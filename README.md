Commit is a 2D pixel art fighting game, Heavily inspired by YOMI Hustle, with my own styles and mechanics.

Just like in the name of the game, the main feature of the game is "Commitment" Where everything has to be finished.
The moment you commit to a combo (e.g M1 combo), you will have to finish the combo or else you'll get a punishment.
No matter if you hit a target or not, you still have to finish the combo, or finish defending, that is not to break your defend too early and get hit in a few moments post blocking.
Theres also a feature i'll be making called "Greed" whereas if you commit too much (via comboing too much) your greed meter start to rise.
Greed decreases overall cooldown of m1's, but in exchange to be easily parried.
Ofcourse I will try to make this as balanced as possible.
Thats why I start thinking features like "Resentment" whereas if you dont hit anything while already doing a combo (especially M1, but we'll see) and keep going to avoid commit punishment, Resentment meter rises and your next hit damage will be multiplied.

Ofcourse I will also try my best to balance this, one of the solution is making it decrease overtime.

There will be other philosophical features in the future. Only time will tell.
As of now I am working on this alone. So be patient.
